1. Voice AI Agent (call handling)
Replaces or augments call centers — answers, books appointments, qualifies leads 24/7. Currently one of the most profitable, less-saturated niches.
Use a voice API (Twilio, Vapi, or similar) + LLM for conversation logic
React/React Native dashboard for call logs and analytics
Sell to clinics, real estate agents, local service businesses 

2. Document/Invoice Processing Agent (B2B, high margin)
Parses PDFs/invoices, extracts data (PO numbers, totals), validates against records, flags mismatches. Every business with paperwork needs this.
Use OCR + LLM extraction, store structured data in MongoDB
Integrate with QuickBooks/Xero/ERP APIs for validation
Charge per-document or tiered enterprise plans — recurring, sticky revenue

3. AI Meeting/Inbox Agent (fast to build, easy sell)
Joins meetings, summarizes, assigns action items, drafts email replies, prioritizes inbox. Businesses already understand the pain point, so sales cycles are short.
Node/Express backend + a calendar/email API (Google, Microsoft Graph) + LLM for summarization
MongoDB to store transcripts/action items; Vercel for deployment
Sell as a monthly SaaS subscription ($15-49/user/month) to small teams

5. AI “Deal Closer” Agent for Service Businesses
(especially agencies, consultants, freelancers, and B2B service providers)
What it does

After a discovery call or sales meeting, the agent:

Transcribes and analyzes the conversation
Automatically generates a tailored proposal + pricing
Sends a personalized follow-up sequence
Handles objections and answers questions via email/Slack/WhatsApp
Books the next meeting or collects the signature/payment when the prospect is ready
